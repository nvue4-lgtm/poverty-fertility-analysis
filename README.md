# Poverty and Fertility Rates Across U.S. States (2007-2024) 
## How has the relationship between poverty and fertility rates across U.S. states changed from 2007 to 2024?

This project uses data from the U.S. Census Bureau's American Community Survey (ACS) 1-year estimates to explore how state-level poverty rates and fertility rates have related to one another over an 18-year period. For each year from 2007–2024 (excluding 2020, when ACS 1-year data wasn't released), the project pulls poverty and fertility rates for all 50 U.S. states, then examines both the year-to-year correlation between the two variables and the overall trend in fertility over time.
